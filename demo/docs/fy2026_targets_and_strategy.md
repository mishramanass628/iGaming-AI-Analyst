## Market priorities
1. **Brazil (BR)** — growth market #1 since regulation (Jan 2025). Target: BR ≥ 20% of group GGR by Q4 2026. Pix is the primary payment method; Pix failure rate must stay under 5%.
2. **Great Britain (GB)** — largest market; protect margin; strict affordability rules apply.
3. **Germany (DE)** — expect pressure: the July 2026 slots stake-limit rule cuts slot turnover. Plan: shift DE players towards Sportsbook and Live Casino. Accepted DE slots turnover decline: up to -40%.
4. **India (IN)** — sportsbook-led (cricket). Monitor UPI deposit failures.
5. Nordics (SE, FI) and NZ — maintain.

## Acquisition
- Affiliates should deliver ≤ 45% of registrations. Any affiliate with FTD conversion below 15% for a quarter goes on a performance review; CPA payments are paid only on verified FTDs.
- Max blended CPA: €110.

## Campaign calendar
- Summer Cashback 2026 (Jul–Aug): 10% cashback on net losses, retention.
- Autumn Spins 2026 (Oct): free-spins reactivation. Budget €70k. Success criteria: +40% October active players vs September, bonus-to-GGR for the campaign month ≤ 25%.
- VIP Reload: ongoing reload bonus for Gold, Platinum and Diamond.
