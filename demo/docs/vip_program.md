# VIP Programme Rules (CRM) — 2026

Tiers are assigned on lifetime turnover (total_stake):
- Bronze: < €1,000
- Silver: €1,000 – €9,999
- Gold: €10,000 – €99,999
- Platinum: €100,000 – €249,999
- Diamond: ≥ €250,000

Benefits: Gold+ get a monthly reload bonus (target cost ≈ 0.5% of monthly turnover), a personal account manager from Platinum, and faster withdrawals (same day) from Gold.

VIP health KPIs:
- VIPs (Gold+) typically generate 60–75% of GGR. Concentration above 80% is a key-person risk and must be flagged to the CFO.
- A Gold+ player inactive for 14 days must get a call from their account manager.
- All Platinum and Diamond players need an annual affordability review (see Responsible Gambling Policy).
